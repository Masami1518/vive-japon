Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The online advice and plan cost you nothing. Only the in-person company is a paid service.

So how do you make money?

Partner venues, experiences and schools pay me a referral fee when I recommend visitors. You pay nothing extra; you actually get perks.

How much is the in-person service?

It depends on the plan (length, places, group size). Mention it in the form and we will discuss it personally.

Which languages do you speak?

Spanish (B1), English (bilingual level) and Japanese (native).

How fast do you reply?

I reply right away once I receive your form.

Do you book hotels or flights?

No. I focus on schools, experiences, restaurants and cafés. You book and pay each place directly, so you always stay in control.

Are you a licensed guide?

I'm a local host: someone who lives in Tokyo and shows you the city like a friend would. I'm not a nationally licensed guide-interpreter.
