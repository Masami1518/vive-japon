Adventure

No.01 · Adventure

Go-kart through Tokyo: Shibuya Crossing and Tokyo Tower

One of the most fun ways to see Tokyo: you drive a kart on real downtown streets, guide in front and your group behind. Perfect with friends or as a couple.

  • Shibuya Crossing, Tokyo Tower and more
  • Guided, small group
  • Photos of your ride included

To drive you need an International Driving Permit (1949 Geneva Convention) or a Japanese licence. Check the activity's requirements before booking.

No.02 · Food & drink

Sake tasting in Asakusa with a specialist

If you want to truly understand sake, this is the one. At WASAKE in the heart of Asakusa, a specialist guides you through up to 10 varieties and explains how they are made and best enjoyed.

  • Up to 10 sakes from 50+ labels
  • Led by a specialist, relaxed atmosphere
  • In Asakusa: pair it with Sensō-ji temple

In Japan you must be 20 or older to drink alcohol.

Things to do in Tokyo

No.03 · Things to do in Tokyo

Live sumo show in Shinjuku (with drink and photo)

The easiest way to see sumo in Tokyo any day of the year. Real bouts a few metres away, the traditions explained, and a photo with the wrestlers. Backed by 1,300+ reviews.

  • Live bouts a few metres away
  • Drink and photo with the wrestlers included
  • In Kabukichō, Shinjuku (75 minutes)

The MC speaks English. If you prefer Spanish, see the Osaka sumo show too.

Day trips from Tokyo

No.04 · Day trips from Tokyo

Nagano snow monkeys: day trip from Tokyo

Watching monkeys bathe in hot springs surrounded by snow is a sight you only get in Japan. You leave from Shinjuku, have beef sukiyaki for lunch and are back in Tokyo by night, no transport worries.

  • Jigokudani Monkey Park
  • Beef sukiyaki lunch
  • Round trip from Shinjuku (about 12 hours)

For snow, December to March is best. You walk a mountain trail to the park: bring comfortable shoes, and warm clothes in winter.

No.05 · Day trips from Tokyo

Mt Fuji day trip from Tokyo

The classic trip to see Fuji up close: Arakurayama pagoda with the mountain behind, the Oshino Hakkai springs and Lake Kawaguchi. 7,000+ reviews and a very accessible price.

  • Arakurayama pagoda with Fuji behind
  • Oshino Hakkai and Lake Kawaguchi
  • Departure from Tokyo (about 10 hours)

Fuji isn't always visible: it depends on the weather. Clear autumn and winter days are usually best.
